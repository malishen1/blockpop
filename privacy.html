Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Block Pop: Combo Puzzle does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your scores, level, unlocked themes and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive or store it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules a local daily reminder on your phone. No data is sent to a server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a request.
