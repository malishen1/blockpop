Block Pop: Combo Puzzle

Drag blocks onto the 8×8 board, fill rows and columns to pop them, and chain clears for big combos. Every point earns XP toward endless levels that unlock new block skins and backgrounds.

How to play

  • Drag the three pieces at the bottom onto the board.
  • Fill a full row or column to clear it.
  • Clear on consecutive moves to build a combo multiplier.
  • Stuck? Use Bomb, Undo or Shuffle.
